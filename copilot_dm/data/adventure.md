# The Shadows of Thornhaven
## A D&D 5E 2024 Solo Adventure (Levels 1-3)

### Campaign Overview
A dark curse has fallen upon the small village of Thornhaven. Livestock have been found dead, drained of blood. Villagers report seeing shadowy figures lurking at the forest's edge. The local herbalist has gone missing. Something evil stirs in the ancient woods, and only a brave hero can uncover the truth and save the village.

**Target Length**: 1.5-2 hours  
**Level Range**: 1-3  
**Tone**: Gothic horror meets heroic fantasy

---

## Act 1: Arrival at Thornhaven (20-30 minutes)

### Scene 1: The Village Square
The player arrives at Thornhaven in the late afternoon. The village is small - maybe 50 residents - clustered around a central square with a well, an inn (The Rusty Nail), a general store, and a small shrine.

**Key Details:**
- The atmosphere is tense - villagers whisper and avoid eye contact
- Two dead sheep are being dragged away, covered in claw marks
- Guard Captain Thorne stands near the well, looking worried

**NPCs to Introduce:**
- **Guard Captain Thorne**: Gruff ex-soldier, missing two fingers on his left hand. Openly discusses the attacks.
- **Innkeeper Mara**: Middle-aged woman with worried eyes. Offers the hero a room and meal in exchange for help.
- **Sage Elric**: Elderly man with wild white hair. Lives in a cottage on the village outskirts. Knows about the forest's history.

**Information to Reveal:**
- Attacks started two weeks ago
- Herbalist Lyssa went into the forest three days ago and hasn't returned
- The attacks happen at night
- Old Elric says the forest has "woken up" - hasn't seen this in 40 years
- Tracks lead north into the Thornwood Forest

**Hook:** Mara offers 50 gp and free lodging for a month if the hero can stop the attacks.

### Scene 2: First Encounter
As evening falls, while the hero is gathering information or preparing:

**Encounter: Wolf Attack** (CR 1/4 x 2)
- Two wolves, driven from the forest by something worse, attack panicked villagers near the well
- This is a tutorial combat - explain action economy clearly
- Wolves should be challenging but not lethal
- After defeating them, Captain Thorne says: "These aren't normal wolves. Look at their eyes - something's driven them mad with fear."

**Rewards:** Gratitude of villagers, healing potions (2) from Mara

**End Act 1:** Hero rests for the night (long rest before Act 2)

---

## Act 2: Into the Thornwood (40-50 minutes)

### Scene 3: The Forest Path
Morning. The hero enters Thornwood Forest following tracks.

**Atmosphere:** Ancient trees, mist, unsettling silence. Occasional animal skull. Growing sense of wrongness.

**Skill Challenge:** 
- Survival DC 12 to follow tracks
- Perception DC 13 to notice goblin ambush

### Scene 4: Goblin Ambush
**Encounter: Goblin Raiders** (CR 1/4 x 3)
- Three goblins jump from bushes
- They're terrified, fleeing from "the spider-things and the big hairy one"
- If questioned (Intimidation DC 11 or Persuasion DC 13): They reveal there's a "cursed cave" deeper in the forest where "the dead walk" and spiders are "big as horses"
- They mention seeing "the plant lady" (Lyssa) dragged toward the cave by zombies

**Loot:** 15 sp, crude map showing the cave location

### Scene 5: The Spider's Lair
Optional encounter if hero explores off the path.

**Encounter: Giant Spider** (CR 1)
- Spider has made a lair in a hollow tree
- Wrapped in webbing: Lyssa's satchel (contains healing herbs - functions as healer's kit with 5 uses)
- This encounter is avoidable with good Stealth

### Scene 6: The Cursed Cave Entrance
The cave entrance is marked with ancient runes (Arcana DC 12: necromantic magic, recently activated).

**Encounter: Zombie Guards** (CR 1/4 x 2)
- Two zombies stand at the entrance
- Recently animated villagers (not from Thornhaven)
- Fight is straightforward but shows the undead threat

**Decision Point:** 
- Enter the cave (continues adventure)
- Return to village for help (Thorne will come, provides advantage on next combat)

---

## Act 3: The Heart of Darkness (30-40 minutes)

### Scene 7: The Cave Depths
The cave descends into a natural cavern lit by eerie blue fungus. Roots dangle from the ceiling. In the center: a crude altar with a dark crystal pulsing with necrotic energy.

**Key NPCs:**
- **Lyssa the Herbalist**: Alive but wounded, held in a cage made of roots
- **Grimfang the Bugbear**: The villain - a bugbear shaman who found the cursed crystal
- **2-3 Zombies**: Thralls

### Scene 8: Confrontation
**Roleplay Opportunity:**
- Grimfang will talk if hero initiates
- He found the crystal and became obsessed with its power
- Plans to raise an army of undead and conquer the region
- Can be convinced to surrender (Persuasion DC 18 - very hard)
- More likely: Combat

**Boss Encounter: Grimfang's Last Stand**
- **Grimfang** (Bugbear - CR 1, but give him 35 HP and +1 to AC)
- **2 Zombies** (CR 1/4 each)
- **Environmental Hazard**: Necrotic crystal deals 1d6 necrotic damage to anyone starting their turn within 5 feet

**Tactics:**
- Zombies engage in melee, trying to grapple
- Grimfang uses his javelin at range, then charges with morningstar
- Surprise Attack if he goes first
- At half HP, he smashes the crystal in desperation

**Crystal Smash:** 
- All zombies collapse (no longer animated)
- Wave of necrotic energy: DC 13 DEX save, 2d6 necrotic damage (half on success)
- Grimfang takes the damage and is weakened (disadvantage on attacks)

**Freeing Lyssa:**
- Cage can be broken (DC 15 STR check or 10 HP damage)
- Lyssa has healing herb poultices (heals 2d4+2)

### Scene 9: Resolution
After defeating Grimfang:

**Immediate:**
- Lyssa thanks the hero profusely
- The cursed crystal is now inert (can be kept as a trophy/sold for 25 gp)
- Search Grimfang's belongings: 40 gp, potion of healing, Grimfang's magic tooth amulet (+1 to initiative)

**Return to Thornhaven:**
- Hero's welcome with the villagers
- Mara provides the promised reward (50 gp) and free lodging
- Captain Thorne offers a badge of honor (advantage on Persuasion with guards in the region)
- Sage Elric identifies the amulet and warns that such dark artifacts sometimes attract more evil - seeds for future adventures
- Lyssa gives the hero her healer's kit and promises to help whenever needed

**Level Up:** If the hero was level 1, they are now level 2. Narrate the growth of power and skill.

**Closing Narration:**
"As the sun sets over Thornhaven, the village is at peace once more. The shadows have been driven back, the cursed dead put to rest. The villagers celebrate their hero with a feast at The Rusty Nail, and for tonight at least, all is well. But you can't help but wonder - if one cursed crystal could cause such havoc, how many more might be hidden in the dark places of the world? Your adventure, brave hero, is only beginning..."

**THE END**

## Encounter Balance Notes

For a **solo level 1 character:**
- Deadly: CR 1/2
- Hard: CR 1/4 x 2
- Medium: CR 1/4 x 1

For a **solo level 2 character:**
- Deadly: CR 1
- Hard: CR 1/2 x 2
- Medium: CR 1/4 x 2

**Adjustments:**
- If player is struggling: Have Thorne arrive as backup, reduce enemy HP
- If player is steamrolling: Add 1 more zombie to final fight, give Grimfang a potion of healing

## DM Tips

1. **Pacing:** If any section runs long, cut one encounter
2. **Death:** If the player dies, Lyssa can save them with rare herbs (once only)
3. **Engagement:** Ask "What are you thinking?" if player seems stuck
4. **Rewards:** Be generous with description and story rewards, moderate with magic items
5. **Next Steps:** Leave hooks - mention rumors of similar attacks in other villages, suggest the crystal came from somewhere
